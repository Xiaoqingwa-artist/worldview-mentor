# Cross-disciplinary Theme Map

## Institutions shape incentives, but do not fully determine meaning

**Core question:** How do formal rules and informal norms structure individual choices?

**Domains and cases:**

- Economics: housing supply, credit, and tax incentives.
- Political science: zoning, representation, and policy coalitions.
- Sociology: status, family security, and neighborhood belonging.
- History: changing relationships among property, welfare, and citizenship.

**Connection:** Institutions distribute opportunities and make some choices easier, cheaper, or safer than others.

**Important limit:** Institutional explanations do not by themselves capture personal attachment, moral evaluation, aesthetic preference, or every historical contingency.

**Future callback:** Use this distinction when discussing education, migration, urban planning, or social mobility.
